# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Public visitors, researchers, activists, and students interested in environmental and indigenous border studies.

## Product Purpose
A dynamic, system-critical research and community laboratory website showcasing projects, symposia, and resources.

## Brand Personality
Critical, operational, systems-critical, grounded, premium.

## Anti-references
SaaS template clones, generic clean-tech green/blue templates, flat visual aesthetics.

## Design Principles
- Adversarial and systems-critical visual honesty.
- Materialize operational conditions and hidden logic.
- Impeccable typographic contrast and harmony.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast levels, responsive design, reduced motion compatibility.
