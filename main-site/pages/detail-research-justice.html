About the Project

This project provides a comprehensive overview of our framework for community-based research praxis, which we term "Research Justice." The initiative features a TEDx talk, a collection of scholarly publications, and a curated list of resources that elaborate on the principles and practices of this approach. Our goal is to promote a research model that is equitable, collaborative, and directly accountable to the communities we work with. By sharing these materials, we hope to inspire and support other researchers and community members in adopting a praxis that honors diverse forms of knowledge and contributes to meaningful social change.
