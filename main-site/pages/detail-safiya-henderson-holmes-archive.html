About the Project

This project is a research justice and critical archival praxis collaboration with the family of the acclaimed Black Arts Movement poet Safiya Henderson-Holmes. The core mission of this initiative is to preserve and digitize her extensive collection of manuscripts, personal papers, and a diverse array of ephemera related to Black feminist and radical movements, as well as the broader arts community. By making these materials accessible, we aim to honor Henderson-Holmes's legacy and provide invaluable resources for scholars, activists, and artists engaged with the intersections of art, feminism, and radical politics.
