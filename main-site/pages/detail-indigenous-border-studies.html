About the Project

This project offers a comprehensive popular education platform that provides thematic modules, scholarly research, readings, films, and opportunities for community dialogue. The initiative is dedicated to centering Indigenous perspectives on critical issues of borders, sovereignty, and movement. Through this work, we aim to foster a deeper understanding of the historical and ongoing impacts of colonial borders on Indigenous communities and to support contemporary movements for self-determination and justice.
