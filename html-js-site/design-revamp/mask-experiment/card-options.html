Project Card Redesign Exploration

Replacing the generic AI left-border stripe with 5 distinct editorial approaches tailored for REGENERACIÓN LAB's archival typography and brand palette.

01

The Editorial Broadside / Index Ledger

Philosophy: Rejects the "floating box" card metaphor entirely. Treats projects as archival entries separated by hair-thin horizontal ledger lines. Color is carried purely through typography and understated bracketed meta kickers. Very clean, scholarly, and print-inspired.

02

The Archival Specimen Slip

Philosophy: Replaces the harsh stripe with a full, whisper-light tinted wash (5% tint of the brand palette) and a matching delicate perimeter hairline. Features an inverted solid accession stamp for new entries. Warm, physical, and artifact-like.

03

The Exhibition Placard (Top Anchor Line)

Philosophy: Moves the color anchor from the side to the top edge (a 3px full-width horizon line), framing the card with a clean 1px neutral enclosure. Aligns the badge and timeline into a balanced top utility row. Architectural, balanced, and authoritative.

04

The Minimalist Typographic Column

Philosophy: Absolute reduction. Zero cards, zero frames, zero drop shadows. Projects breathe freely directly on the wash background, anchored by an understated bullet kicker and larger display titles. Ultra-modern editorial magazine aesthetic.
