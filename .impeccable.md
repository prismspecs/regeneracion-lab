## Design Context

### Users
Academic researchers, Indigenous community members, and students. They are looking for authoritative but community-grounded research on water justice and decolonial media. The job to be done is consuming dense research materials, exploring thematic modules, and understanding the lab's mission.

### Brand Personality
Confidence, Refined, and Community-Grounded.
- **3-Word Personality**: Editorial, Anarchist, Refined.
- **Emotional Goals**: Confidence, Authority, Clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: "Turn-of-the-century anarchist newspaper" meets "Modern Apple/Kinfolk-style editorial". High-contrast "ink-on-paper" feel.
- **References**: Kinfolk (for whitespace, typography hierarchy, and editorial layout).
- **Theme**: Newspaper Style (High Contrast) with off-white/paper backgrounds and deep ink-black typography. Nature-evoking accents (silty greens, river blues) in OKLCH.

### Design Principles
1. **Editorial Authority**: Use bold, high-contrast typography (Instrument Serif) to establish confidence and a sense of "The News".
2. **Intentional Whitespace**: Adopt Kinfolk-style generous margins and breathing room to make dense academic content approachable.
3. **Sharp & Brutalist**: No rounded corners. Use sharp borders and clean lines to reflect a modernist/anarchist aesthetic.
4. **River-Inspired Palette**: Use perceptually uniform OKLCH colors for accents that evoke the silty, flowing nature of water justice themes.
5. **SPA Simplicity**: Maintain the HTML-first architecture to ensure the site remains "light" and migratable to WordPress.
